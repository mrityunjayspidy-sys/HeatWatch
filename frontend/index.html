About HeatWatch AI

How it Works

HeatWatch AI is a two-stage machine learning platform for urban heat island analysis and mitigation:

Stage A — Predictive Digital Twin

Features two viable model architectures trained with Spatial District Block-Split validation across 65,207 real satellite grid cells:

  • Option 1 (Tabular / Gradient-Boosted): Random Forest & HistGradientBoosting Ensemble (94.1% R²).
  • Option 2 (Deep Learning Neural Net / CNN): Multi-layer Neural Network / Spatial Patch Regressor (94.3% R²).

Stage B — Prescriptive GA Optimizer

A Genetic Algorithm (GA) evolves optimal intervention maps (trees, cool roofs, water bodies, shade canopies) under budget constraints and equity/vulnerability weighting.
